### 2. Rule Fidelity Audit (Tyrants of the Underdark)

#### Control vs. Total Control (`SiteControlSystem.cs`)

* **The Critique:** I see logic for counting troops, but the "Spy" interaction is often a source of bugs.
* **Fidelity Check:** Total Control is defined as: *All troop spaces filled with your troops AND no enemy spies.* 
* **Observation:** Ensure your `SiteControlSystem` re-evaluates Total Control whenever a `PlaceSpyCommand` or `ReturnSpyCommand` is executed. If your `MapNode` doesn't explicitly track the presence of spies in its `ControlState` calculation, you are violating Page 15 of the rulebook.

### 3. Technical Debt & Performance (MonoGame Specifics)

#### Memory Allocations (GC Pressure)

* **The Critique:** `DtoMapper.cs` and your various `Update` loops appear to create new objects/lists frequently.
* **Cold Truth:** In a competitive game, a "GC Hitch" (stutter) during a critical animation is unacceptable.
* **The Fix:** Use **Object Pooling** for `ExecutedAction` objects and avoid `Linq` (like `.ToList()` or `.Select()`) inside the `Draw` or `Update` paths of `MapRenderer` and `CardRenderer`.

#### Input Polling vs. Events

* **The Critique:** `MonoGameInputProvider` uses polling.
* **The Fix:** For a UI-heavy game, you should wrap polling in an **Event-Driven wrapper**. This prevents "Double-Clicks" from being registered across frames if the frame rate dips.
